Our Real Story

IT ALL STARTED WITH A SIMPLE BUS JOURNEY...

Before that fateful day, we were practically strangers who hadn't spoken a single word. Then an ordinary college route wrote the first sentence of our forever.

Chapter 01

Two Strangers

We were practically strangers before we started talking. Two separate lives sharing the same bus route day after day, not knowing what the universe had in store for us.

Chapter 02

One College Bus Journey

On an ordinary morning ride, amid the quiet hum of the college bus, our paths finally crossed. A simple turn of events turned an everyday commute into the most meaningful journey of my life.

Chapter 03

An Internship Doubt

I had a doubt about an internship and decided to approach you. What started as an academic question was really destiny giving us the gentle push we needed to begin.

Chapter 04

The First Conversation

That first conversation was effortless and genuine. Your warmth immediately put me at ease, and those few initial minutes felt so natural, like we had known each other long before.

Chapter 05

Friendship Begins

One conversation turned into daily check-ins. With every bus ride, we found ourselves smiling more, talking about our dreams, and discovering a rare, effortless companionship.

Chapter 06

Something Beautiful Develops

She was a fourth-year senior, and I was a third-year student. Yet throughout our journey, I never felt that senior-junior difference. What truly mattered was our pure love, mutual respect, and profound connection.

Chapter 07

Our Love Story

As the days passed, our talks became the sweetest part of my day, and our friendship naturally blossomed into love. Today, you are my sanctuary, my happiness, and my favourite story.

ONE LETTER. A THOUSAND FEELINGS.

Written from the heart by G. Selvaganapathy, waiting for your touch.

To the girl who became my safest place… ❤️

Divya Priya,

Honestly, I've met so many people in this world, but I've never come across someone who makes me feel the way you do.

Do you remember how everything started? It was just an ordinary college bus journey. I started talking to you because I had a doubt about an internship. We were practically strangers at that time. A simple conversation slowly became friendship, and that friendship eventually turned into something beautiful.

You were my senior, and I was your junior, but honestly, I never felt that difference between us. All I ever felt was the love, understanding, and connection we shared.

You know what makes you so different to me?

Your understanding.

The way you understand me, the way you listen to me, the way you make me feel safe enough to be myself — I've never experienced anything quite like that.

Even when you have your own family problems and difficulties, you still find ways to stand by me. When I need you, you try to be there. I don't think you realize how much those little things mean to me.

I don't expect anything extraordinary from you. Your presence itself means so much to me.

Sometimes I wonder how I got so lucky to find someone like you. I will always be grateful that a simple conversation on a college bus brought you into my life.

I never want you to feel that your efforts, your love, or your presence go unnoticed. I want to take care of you, understand you, support you, and give you the same comfort you've given me.

I don't know exactly what every tomorrow will look like, but one thing I genuinely wish for is to keep choosing each other, growing together, and sharing this beautiful journey for a lifetime.

You are not just someone I love. You are someone whose presence has become an irreplaceable part of my life.

No one can replace what you mean to me.

Happy Birthday, my Divya Priya. ❤️

I love you. More than these words can ever explain.
Forever yours, G. Selvaganapathy ❤️
